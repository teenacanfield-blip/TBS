THE ART BOOK

Every colour in the game, pulled live out of game.js as you load this page — nothing here is a copy, so none of it can drift from what you actually see when you play.

Sprites are four-tone character grids pointed at a ramp, the way the handhelds did it: one grid, many palettes. So a creature is drawn twice over — the animal in a real fur, feather or shell colour, and the object possessing it glowing in that object's own material. A line's three stages are the same coat lifted towards the light or pushed down into it; an ultra rare is that coat nudged round the colour wheel, a legendary the other way, and a shiny is the whole thing walked most of the way round.

THE LEDGER WHEEL

Ten types, ten ramps. Each type overpowers the two that follow it on the wheel and struggles against the two before it — the same four colours are used for that type's moves, badges, marks and the glow coming off anything it possessed.

THE ROSTER

Forty-four species, one generation, all catchable.

SHINY COATS

Rolled on every wild Roaminal, about one in two hundred — doubled by the Bright Penny in the boiler room.

MAX, AND THE STAFF

People get a seven-slot ramp instead of a creature's four, because a face needs skin, hair and cloth that do not share a colour.

THE TEN FLOORS

One palette per floor, and every tile on it drawn from that palette rather than stored as a picture.
