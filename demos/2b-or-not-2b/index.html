2B or not 2B

You rolled off the desk. It is a long way back up.

Run, jump, and stab your tip into anything soft enough to hold it — cork, vinyl, chewing gum, the back of a drawer. Sharpeners are the only way to get your point back, and they are the only checkpoints. Falling will not kill you. It will just put you on the floor again.

    Controls

    W A S DRun, in whatever direction the camera is facing
    MouseLook. Click once to hold the camera
    SpaceJump. Again in the air for a spin, once per flight
    Space at a wallKick off it, if your tip is in it
    Shift / EDash. One per flight, back when you land
    Ctrl / Q / FDive tip-first. Deadly on erasers, in the good way
    RBack to the last sharpener
    MMute
    EscPause

    Hold toward a soft wall in mid-air and the pencil sticks into it. It slides, it costs sharpness, and at zero sharpness nothing holds — so the sharpeners are worth going out of your way for.
